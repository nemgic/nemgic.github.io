Politika privatnosti

Aplikacija: Fiddle Programer: nemgic com.grgic.fiddle Poslednja izmena: 14. septembar 2026.

Pre objavljivanja: zameniti adresu za kontakt na dnu stranice pravom adresom. Ovo obaveštenje se uklanja pre unosa adrese u Google Play Console.

Fiddle ne prikuplja nikakve podatke o vama. Nema server, nema korisnički nalog, i ništa ne napušta vaš uređaj — osim tokom opcione, direktne veze sa uređajima vaših članova benda na istoj Wi-Fi mreži.

Sažetak

Fiddle je pesmarica sa akordima za muzičare: biblioteka pesama, transpozicija, kapo, dijagrami akorda, metronom, setliste, režim nastupa sa automatskim skrolovanjem i sesija uživo za usklađivanje članova benda. Aplikacija nema server, nema korisničke naloge i nema prijavu. Sama aplikacija nikada ne šalje nijedan mrežni zahtev ka internetu.

Koje podatke prikupljamo

Nikakve. Fiddle nema analitiku, telemetriju, reklamne SDK-ove ni prijavljivanje grešaka trećim stranama. Programer ne prima, ne vidi i ne čuva nikakve vaše podatke.

Gde se čuvaju vaši podaci

Sve se čuva isključivo lokalno na vašem uređaju, u lokalnoj bazi podataka: pesme (tekst i akordi), plejliste i setliste, bendovi sa imenima i instrumentima članova, podešavanja i beleške. Deinstalacija aplikacije briše sve ove podatke.

Dozvole i zašto su potrebne

android.permission.INTERNET Internet

Ne koristi se za pristup internetu. Android traži ovu dozvolu za svaku mrežnu vezu, uključujući i vezu na lokalnoj Wi-Fi mreži, koja se koristi samo za funkciju Sesija uživo opisanu ispod.

android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Mikrofon

Koristi se isključivo za jednokratno merenje kašnjenja zvuka uređaja. Aplikacija pušta niz kratkih tonova i meri kada ih mikrofon čuje, da bi metronom kliknuo u istom trenutku na svim telefonima. Zvuk se ne snima, ne čuva i ne šalje nigde — meri se samo vreme. Dozvolu dajete kada pokrenete kalibraciju i možete je uskratiti; sve ostalo u aplikaciji radi bez mikrofona.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Servis u prvom planu

Da metronom nastavi da svira kada se ekran ugasi tokom nastupa.

android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Izmena audio podešavanja

Potrebno za reprodukciju metronoma i zvuka akorda.

android.permission.VIBRATE Vibracija

Haptički odziv na dodir.

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Pristup fajlovima — samo Android 12 i stariji

Ograničeno na maxSdkVersion=32. Potrebno da biste izabrali svoj fajl za uvoz pesme i sačuvali izvezeni PDF.

Sesija uživo — jedina mrežna funkcija

Članovi benda na istoj Wi-Fi mreži mogu direktno povezati svoje uređaje. Razmenjuju se pesma koja se trenutno svira, ton, kapo, tempo i signal za zajednički klik metronoma. Ovi podaci ne napuštaju lokalnu mrežu i ne prolaze kroz nijedan server. Pridruživanje sesiji je dobrovoljno i traje samo dok je držite otvorenom.

Uvoz pesama

Sami birate svoje fajlove kroz sistemski birač fajlova. Fajl se čita lokalno na uređaju i nikada se ne šalje nigde. Aplikacija ne preuzima sadržaj pesama sa interneta.

Izvoz i deljenje

Pesmu možete izvesti kao PDF, običan tekst ili ChordPro, a celu biblioteku kao JSON fajl, kroz sistemski meni za deljenje. Odredište birate sami; aplikacija ne šalje ništa samoinicijativno.

Android rezervna kopija

Fiddle dozvoljava Android automatsku rezervnu kopiju, pa operativni sistem može da napravi rezervnu kopiju podataka aplikacije na vaš Google Drive nalog. To radi Android, ne aplikacija, i podleže Google-ovim uslovima korišćenja. Ovo možete isključiti u podešavanjima Androida.

Deljenje sa trećim stranama

Ne delimo podatke ni sa kim, jer ih ne prikupljamo.

Bezbednost

Pošto svi podaci ostaju na vašem uređaju i ne šalju se nama, ne postoje vaši podaci na serveru koje bi trebalo štititi. Standardna Android izolacija aplikacija štiti vaše lokalne podatke kao i kod svake druge aplikacije na uređaju.

Deca

Fiddle nije namenjen deci mlađoj od 13 godina i ne prikuplja podatke ni od koga, uključujući decu.

Vaša prava i kontrola nad podacima

Pošto se svi podaci čuvaju samo na vašem uređaju, potpuno ste u kontroli: u svakom trenutku možete pregledati, izmeniti, izvesti ili obrisati svoje pesme, plejliste i bendove unutar aplikacije, a deinstalacija aplikacije trajno briše sve podatke.

Privacy Policy

App: Fiddle Developer: nemgic com.grgic.fiddle Last updated: 14 September 2026

Before publishing: replace the contact address at the bottom of this page with a real one. This notice is removed before the URL goes into Google Play Console.

Fiddle does not collect any data about you. There is no server, no account, and nothing leaves your device — except during an optional, direct connection to your own band members' devices on the same Wi-Fi network.

Summary

Fiddle is a chord book for musicians: a song library, transposition, capo, chord diagrams, a metronome, setlists, a performance mode with auto-scroll, and a live session for keeping a band in sync. The app has no backend server, no user accounts, and no login. The app itself never sends a single network request to the internet.

What data we collect

None. Fiddle has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party crash reporting. The developer does not receive, see, or store any of your data.

Where your data lives

Everything is stored locally on your device, in a local database: your songs (lyrics and chords), playlists and setlists, bands with member names and instruments, settings, and notes. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Permissions and why they are needed

android.permission.INTERNET Internet

Not used to reach the internet. Android requires this permission for any network connection, including one on a local Wi-Fi network, which is the only thing it is used for — the Live Session feature described below.

android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO Microphone

Used solely for a one-time measurement of the device's audio latency. The app plays a series of short tones and measures when the microphone hears them, so the metronome can click at the same instant on every phone. Audio is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere — only timing is measured. You grant this permission when you start the calibration and may decline it; everything else in the app works without the microphone.

android.permission.FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK Foreground service

Lets the metronome keep playing when the screen turns off during a performance.

android.permission.MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS Modify audio settings

Required to play the metronome and chord sounds.

android.permission.VIBRATE Vibration

Haptic feedback on taps.

READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE File access — Android 12 and older only

Limited to maxSdkVersion=32. Needed so you can pick your own file to import a song and save an exported PDF.

Live Session — the only network feature

Band members on the same Wi-Fi network can connect their devices directly. They exchange the song currently being played, the key, capo, tempo, and a shared metronome click signal. This data never leaves the local network and never passes through any server. Joining a session is voluntary and lasts only as long as you keep it open.

Importing songs

You pick your own files through the system file picker. The file is read locally on your device and is never sent anywhere. The app does not download song content from the internet.

Export and sharing

You can export a song as a PDF, plain text, or ChordPro, and your whole library as a JSON file, through the system share menu. You choose the destination; the app never sends anything on its own.

Android backup

Fiddle allows Android's automatic backup, so the operating system may back up the app's data to your own Google Drive account. This is done by Android, not by the app, and is governed by Google's terms. You can turn it off in your Android settings.

Sharing with third parties

We do not share data with anyone, because we do not collect any.

Security

Since all data stays on your device and none is transmitted to us, there is no data of yours on a server to protect. Standard Android app sandboxing protects your local data as it does for any other app on the device.

Children

Fiddle is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Your rights and control over your data

Because everything is stored only on your device, you are always in full control: you can view, edit, export, or delete your songs, playlists, and bands at any time inside the app, and uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.
